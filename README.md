# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 10,000 e-commerce sales records using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional trends, and the impact of discounts.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales and profit across categories
- Analyze regional performance
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard

## Dataset

- **Records:** 10,000
- **Period:** January 2024 – December 2025
- **Data includes:** Order ID, Order Date, Customer ID, Product, Category, Region, Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Sales, Cost and Profit

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- SQL (Basic)

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared and analyzed using Excel and Power BI. Data types were checked, data quality was reviewed, and calculated fields and DAX measures were created for analysis.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Cost
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin %

## Power BI Dashboard

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides an overall view of sales, profit, cost, orders and quantity, along with sales and profit trends, category performance and regional profitability.

### 2. Product Analysis

Analyzes top products by sales and profit, category performance, profit margin and quantity sold.

### 3. Discount & Profitability

Examines the relationship between discounts and profit margin, total profit, and profitability across regions and categories.

## Key Insights

- Total Sales: **₹228.45M**
- Total Profit: **₹59.65M**
- Electronics is the strongest category in terms of sales and profit.
- Regional profitability is relatively balanced, with some variation between regions.
- Higher discount levels are associated with lower profit margins.
- Profit margin decreases from approximately **31.72% at 0% discount to 14.70% at 20% discount**.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor high discount levels to protect profit margins.
- Focus on high-performing products and categories.
- Investigate lower-performing products to identify improvement opportunities.
- Monitor regional profitability regularly.
- Use controlled discounting strategies to balance sales growth and profitability.
